About Our Company

Driving Corporate Success Through Reliable Mobility

Classic Travels Solutions India Pvt Ltd embarked on its journey in 2012, aiming to redefine corporate transportation. Over a decade of relentless service and dedication led to our incorporation as a Private Limited entity in 2021.

We are a growing, specialized employee transportation and reliable fleet management company proudly serving major corporate hubs in Mumbai and Pune. Our commitment lies in safe, punctual, and comfortable transit for the modern workforce.
